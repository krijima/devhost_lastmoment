# Design

Shadow should feel calm and clear when the surrounding page applies pressure. Use warm ivory, forest green, pale lime, restrained borders, and readable system typography. The demo uses Manrope/DM Sans when available.

The playground frames a fictional streaming account in a browser-like canvas. Three independent product promises: see potential pressure, decide explicitly, keep evidence.

The assistant sits at bottom right in an isolated dark panel. It offers goal selection, page scan, candidate actions, findings/evidence tabs, highlight links, and local JSON export. It collapses or closes. Approval appears above observations with the exact button label and destination hostname.

Status messages use an aria-live region. Buttons have accessible labels and visible focus outlines. Panels scroll within the viewport. Mobile layouts stack content. Accessibility remains subject to real assistive-technology testing.

Copy must label heuristic uncertainty, avoid unverifiable legal assurances, and distinguish a click from a confirmed outcome.
