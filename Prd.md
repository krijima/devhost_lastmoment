# Shadow product requirements

## Purpose
Help consumers recognize manipulative interfaces and complete their intended cancellation, privacy, or refund journey with explicit control.

## MVP delivered
- Chrome/Edge Manifest V3 extension activated by toolbar click.
- Floating, collapsible assistant with goal selection and live DOM observation.
- Heuristic detection of confirm-shaming, urgency, retention offers, auto-renewal, small refusal controls, and preselected optional consent.
- Highlight findings; find candidate actions; approve or decline each click.
- In-memory timestamped evidence and JSON export.
- Fictional multistep cancellation playground.

## Acceptance
No assisted click without explicit approval. Changed pages invalidate pending approval. No passwords, form values, cookies, or payment credentials collected. Findings explain uncertainty. Completion requires checking the merchant confirmation.

## Not yet delivered
Model reasoning, screenshots, tamper-evident evidence, cross-page session persistence, verified refunds or claims, regulator integrations, accessibility certification, cross-origin frame and closed-shadow traversal.

## Evaluation
Use identical randomized scenarios for manual and assisted users. Record completion rate, time, clicks, mistaken outcomes, false positives, false negatives, and approval violations. No measured performance claims yet.
